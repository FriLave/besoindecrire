Choices, sacrifices, strengths, dreams, wounds, victories.

Giving her a biography is a way of telling her:

  • "You have a journey, a voice, a story that deserves to be written."
  • "I want to know it, understand it, pass it on."

It is a gift that recognises the woman, not just the maternal figure.
