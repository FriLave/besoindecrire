Some encounters begin with a simple click. A reader lands on my blog, often by chance, sometimes guided by an intuition, a search, or a wish to understand how words can shed light on a life. This first contact is discreet, almost silent. And yet it already opens a door.

This blog is not a showcase. It is a place to breathe. Here the reader discovers a voice, a rhythm, a way of looking at the world. And, without knowing it yet, they begin to walk a path.

From reader to traveller: when words become a passage

Reading is already a kind of journey. As the reader moves through the texts, they recognise something: a sensitivity, a way of listening, a way of connecting stories to people. They understand that behind every article there is an intention: to bring out what deserves to be said.

This is the moment the reader becomes a traveller. They begin to picture it. They wonder what their own story would become, put into words, brought to light, given structure, treated with respect. They catch themselves imagining what they might confide, what they might pass on, what they might keep for themselves.

This passage is essential. It marks the beginning of a bond.
